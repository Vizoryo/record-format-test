What it does — Northlake Plumbing

Northlake Plumbing repairs and replaces plumbing in homes and small rental buildings in Austin, Round Rock and Pflugerville, Texas. It finds and fixes leaks, clears blocked drains and installs gas and electric water heaters, standard and tankless, and gives a fixed price before work starts. It works at the customer's property and has no walk-in shop.

  • Customers served: homeowners, landlords
  • How it serves: visits customers
  • Specialties: tankless water heaters, slab leak detection
