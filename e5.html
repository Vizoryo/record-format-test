What it does — Ledgerline Payroll

Ledgerline Payroll sells online payroll software for small businesses in the United States with 1–50 employees. It runs payroll, pays staff by direct deposit, files federal and state payroll taxes, and moves a company's year-to-date payroll over from another provider. It works fully online and has no office open to visitors.
